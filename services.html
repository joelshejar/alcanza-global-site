Our business activities

Bringing together the moving parts of trade.

International sourcing is more than finding a product. We help buyers start clear conversations about their requirements and the partners who can support them.

What we do

Connected services for commercial needs.

Services are subject to available partners, jurisdictional requirements and individual project scope.

01

Import–Export Trading

We handle agricultural commodity buying and selling discussions, from product matching and pricing through contract terms and shipment coordination.

02

International Procurement & Sourcing

We shortlist supply options by grade, quantity, packaging and destination, then coordinate samples and specification checks before you commit.

03

Freight & Logistics Booking

We arrange freight and logistics with shipping providers for agreed transactions and keep you updated on vessel and delivery schedules.

04

Export Documentation

We prepare and coordinate the documents required for each shipment, including invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin and phytosanitary or lab reports where applicable.

05

Inspection Coordination

We arrange agreed pre-shipment quality checks and independent inspections, then share the resulting reports with the buyer.

06

Customs Clearance Coordination

We coordinate with licensed customs professionals so export and import formalities are handled correctly for the destination market.

07

Packaging & Labelling

We confirm buyer-specific pack sizes, bag or carton type, labelling and marking requirements with the supplier before production.

08

Warehousing & Consolidation

Where required, we arrange storage and consolidation through service partners so multiple items can move under a coordinated shipment plan.

09

Buyer & Supplier Coordination

We keep all parties aligned on specifications, timelines, documents and payment terms until the shipment is delivered.

10

General Trading & Commodity Supply

We explore trade opportunities across our core portfolio and suitable additional commodity categories on request.

How we work

Clear requirements create better conversations.

Tell us your product, specifications, intended market and delivery preferences. We can then discuss an appropriate commercial next step.

  • Product and grade clarification
  • Packaging and volume preferences
  • Destination and trade-term discussions
  • Sourcing availability assessment
  • Partner coordination where relevant
How we work

Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Some useful context about our products, sourcing model and how to begin a business enquiry.

Can I purchase products directly from the website?

The website is a business-to-business catalogue, not a checkout store. Share your requirements through Request a Quote to begin a commercial discussion.

Which products can I enquire about?

Our initial portfolio includes red chillies, green chillies, onions, avocados, cashews, chickpeas, green mung beans, cloves, black pepper, basmati rice and Kaima (Jeerakasala) rice.

Do you have a standard minimum order quantity?

Minimum order quantities differ by product, sourcing partner, pack type and shipment route. Please include your expected quantity in your enquiry.

Which countries do your products come from?

Alcanza Global follows a multi-country sourcing model. The available country of origin depends on the commodity and the applicable sourcing partner; it will be confirmed for the transaction.

Are product grades and certifications available?

Relevant product specifications and documents can be discussed on a case-by-case basis. Please specify required grades, tests or certifications in your enquiry. No certification is guaranteed by website content alone.

Can you coordinate shipping and packaging?

Packaging preferences and logistics coordination may be discussed through relevant partners, subject to feasibility and agreed commercial terms.

Do you work with buyers outside the GCC?

Yes. Our target audience includes buyers across India, Africa, Europe, Southeast Asia and other international markets, subject to applicable trade and regulatory requirements.

How can I request a quotation?

Use the Request a Quote form, providing your company details, commodity, intended quantity, packaging preferences and destination. Direct form delivery requires the site owner to configure a business email or Formspree endpoint.

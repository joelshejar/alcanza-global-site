Privacy Policy

How information submitted through this website may be collected and used.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This website is presented by Alcanza Global. This policy describes how information may be handled when you browse the site or contact us. It should be reviewed against the company's actual operations and local legal requirements before public launch.

1. Information you provide

When you submit a business enquiry, information may include your name, company, business email, telephone number, country, requested product, quantity, shipping destination and message. We use this information to review and respond to your enquiry and manage related business communication.

2. How forms work

This is a static website. Depending on the site's configuration, submitting an enquiry may open your email application, or send the form through an external form-processing provider (such as Formspree). If no submission service is configured, the site will explain that submission is unavailable. Form-processing services have their own data practices and policies.

3. WhatsApp and email

Contacting us through WhatsApp or email involves using those third-party platforms. Their privacy policies also apply to communications on those services.

4. Analytics

Optional website analytics may be enabled by the site owner. If enabled, any applicable privacy notices, consent mechanisms and legal requirements should be implemented.

5. Sharing and retention

Business enquiry information may be shared with service providers or relevant sourcing partners when reasonably needed to evaluate your request, subject to applicable law and commercial confidentiality. Retention should be limited to legitimate business needs and legal obligations.

6. Your enquiries

For questions about your information, please use the Contact Us page. Applicable access, correction or deletion rights depend on relevant data-protection law.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website, systems or operating practices change.

Who we are

Trade built on relationships.

Alcanza Global is a global import-export trading company with a commitment to clear communication, thoughtful sourcing and meaningful commercial partnerships.

Our story

A world connected by commerce.

We believe international trade works best when buyers and sourcing partners understand each other’s expectations from the beginning.

Alcanza Global is an import-export trading company working with buyers in India and international markets. We source agricultural commodities and food products through a partner network and support each enquiry from specification and sampling through documentation and shipment.

We make the procurement journey clearer by paying attention to product specifications, intended use, packaging, trade terms and the needs of each buyer.

Our approach is simple: conduct business professionally, communicate with clarity and work toward relationships that last beyond an individual order.
